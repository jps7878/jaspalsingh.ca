# jaspalsingh.ca — design spec

Date: 2026-09-08. Status: approved direction, building.

## Purpose

A one-page personal brand site for Jaspal Singh. Audience: people who look him up (colleagues, recruiters, founders, Anthropic peers). A visitor should leave thinking "Incentives and revenue-operations operator who sold first, then built the systems, and now engineers them, with taste." Concept: an homage to Yu-Gi-Oh trading cards. Every career role is rendered as an original, physically convincing card. The copy stays plain and professional; the whimsy lives in the object.

## Decisions already made

- Hosting: hand-written static site on GitHub Pages, repo `jps7878/jaspalsingh.ca`, deploy from `main`. Domain stays at Network Solutions; A records plus a `www` CNAME point at GitHub Pages.
- No framework, no dependencies. A zero-dependency Node script (`build.js`) renders `index.html` from `content.json` and templates, and the rendered `index.html` is committed. GitHub Pages serves the committed file; nothing builds on deploy.
- Cards are done for real (owner's choice after rejecting flat CSS boxes): textured brushed-metal frames with chamfer and molded ridge, engraved name plate, struck-coin attribute badge, recessed art window with metal bezel, paper-grain parchment effect box, set code and copyright in the bottom strip, pointer-driven 3D tilt, and on the two current roles a light-following holographic foil. The reference implementation is `reference/index.html` (two-card proof, approved 2026-09-08).
- Backdrop (chosen 2026-09-08 from four judged variants) is a honed blue-grey slate slab: procedural stone mottle and fine grain, one key light from the upper left, bottom vignette. Chrome on it is cool off-white ink with steel links. Card shadows fall down-right to agree with the light. No grid, no pattern, no second light source.
- No level stars, no ATK/DEF, no self-rating of any kind. No card-style portrait in the hero. Section headings are plain words.
- Company logos appear on every card as a monochrome mark set into the art window (owner's request). Company names and marks belong to their owners; the footer says so.
- Artwork is AI-generated by the owner from `~/Desktop/jaspalsingh-card-art-prompts.md`, saved to `assets/art/art-0N-<company>.jpg` at 1:1. Until a file exists, the card shows a code-drawn SVG placeholder in the same slot. Swapping art is a file drop, not a code change.

## Page structure

1. Hero (revised 2026-09-08 evening at the owner's request: "description plus picture"). Left: name, headline, one-line tagline ("I build the systems that decide how sellers get paid."), four-sentence bio, links (LinkedIn, Email, Qotiv, GitHub) rendered as struck steel coins built like the card badges (reeded rim, spun face, mark engraved in the ENG ink) with the label beside each; footer repeats them at 30px. Icons in assets/icons (LinkedIn and GitHub marks link only to the owner's own profiles). Left-aligned, max 64ch. Right: a large portrait (`assets/photo.jpg`, shown at 4:5 about 360px wide, rounded, grounded shadow; a neutral initials tile until the photo exists). On phones the portrait sits above the name at 120-160px. No card in the hero.
2. Experience. Heading "Experience". All seven cards, newest first, three per row on wide screens (about 430px wide, rows of 3/3/1 left-aligned), two columns on tablets, one column on phones (about 340px). Cards keep the 59:86 ratio at every size.
3. Skills. Heading "Skills", a one-line plain-words intro ("Spells are what I design and build. Traps are the controls that keep payouts honest."), and one full-bleed horizontal shelf of eight FULL cards (same anatomy and size as the career cards) in the spell (green) and trap (magenta) metals, matte. Order (2026-09-09): the GTM Engineer rare first, then the compensation block (Comp Plan Design, Payout Controls, Comp Compliance, Data Integrity), then GTM in journey order (GTM Strategy, Funnel Mapping, Quota & Territory, Account Management), Data Analysis last. Shelf: scroll-snap, first card aligned to the content column, peek of the next card, prev/next buttons (JS, hidden without it), keyboard-scrollable region. Cards sit straight (a hand-laid tilt was tried and reverted on 2026-09-09). Skill cards differ from career cards only in: a glyph coin (spell sparkle, trap hexagonal ring) instead of a word, type line `[ Spell / Design & Strategy ]` or `[ Trap / Controls & Governance ]`, no company mark. Set codes continue JS-EN008 to JS-EN015; each has its own art slot with a code-drawn placeholder until real art exists (optional prompts in the owner's art document).
4. Footer. One line styled like card fine print: set code, copyright, ownership note, and "Designed and built with Claude Code" linking to claude.com/claude-code (owner wants the Claude use stated plainly). Same four links.

## Card system

Frame types (all derived from the proof's material stack: base gradient, directional light, grain tile, brushed tile, 2px dark edge, 1px chamfer, molded ridge, top-lit inner edge):

| frame   | roles                          | base metal            | badge word | badge face   |
|---------|--------------------------------|-----------------------|------------|--------------|
| eng     | Anthropic                      | brushed silver-white  | ENG        | dark slate   |
| founder | Qotiv                          | brushed violet        | FOUNDER    | deep violet  |
| ops     | Engine, Deel RevOps            | brushed copper-orange | OPS        | deep blue    |
| sales   | Deel AE, Clio, RBC Insurance   | brushed gold-tan      | SALES      | deep crimson |
| spell   | design & strategy skills       | brushed green         | glyph      | deep forest  |
| trap    | controls & governance skills   | brushed magenta       | glyph      | deep plum    |
| rare    | GTM Engineer (the set's one Secret Rare, added 2026-09-09) | royal blue, dark plate, light ink, FOIL | star glyph | deep navy |

Anatomy (career cards), top to bottom: name plate about 9% of height with the role title in Cormorant SC and the coin at right; art window about 50% of height, inset about 7% per side, recessed; type line `[ Company / City / Dates ]` in bold small serif; effect box about 20% with a paragraph in EB Garamond; bottom strip with set code left and copyright right. Long titles are condensed horizontally to stay on one line, as real cards do.

Logo: a monochrome white mark, about 13% of card width, bottom-right inside the art window, with a soft shadow so it reads on any art. On foil cards the foil passes over it like everything else in the window.

Foil (eng and founder only): one light-following rainbow band with a near-white specular core, hard-light blended, masked so it is full strength on the frame, most of the strength on the plate, about a third over the art, and nearly nothing over the parchment. Directional micro-grain confined to the band. At rest the band sits at a fixed angle so the card reads as foil without a pointer. Non-foil cards get a subtle satin band in their own metal.

Tilt: pointer-driven, up to 10 degrees on foil cards and 6 on matte, perspective 1000px, eases back on leave. Disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` and on touch devices (cards render at rest). The page is complete with JavaScript disabled.

## Content

All copy lives in `content.json`. Career text is a paragraph per card, professional, no jokes. Skills are name plus one line. Copy for RBC, Clio, Deel AE, Qotiv and Anthropic is the owner's draft and may be edited in place.

## Typography and color

Cormorant SC for card names, EB Garamond for card text, Inter for the small labels inside cards (badge words, bottom strip). Page chrome (revised 2026-09-08 late, owner found Inter bland): Space Grotesk for the name, tagline and section headings; Plus Jakarta Sans for body text, links, footer. Google Fonts, one link, `display=block`. Page ink for the hero is a warm off-white on the charcoal; links a muted silver. All saturated color lives in the cards.

## Quality floor

- Responsive from 360px to 1600px with no horizontal scroll.
- Text contrast at least 4.5:1 everywhere (parchment ink, hero text, bottom strip).
- Visible keyboard focus on links. Semantic landmarks and headings; each card is an `article` with the role as `h3`.
- Reduced motion respected; JS-off renders complete.
- Weight budget: HTML under 250KB, logos under 20KB each, art under 400KB each after the owner exports them.
- Verified by headless Chrome screenshots at 1440 and 390 widths before every commit.

## Out of scope

Blog, analytics, contact form, side-project cards, dark/light toggle (the site is dark by design), gyroscope tilt on phones.
