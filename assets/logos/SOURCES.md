# Logo sources

All marks are rendered white for use inside card art windows. Trademarks belong to their owners.

- anthropic.svg: Simple Icons (CC0) rendition of the Anthropic mark.
- qotiv.png: cropped mark from the Qotiv logo (source file kept at reference/qotiv-source.png, owned by Jaspal Singh; not served from assets/).
- engine.png: derived from engine.com favicon (bars only).
- deel.png: Wikimedia Commons "DEEL-new-logo.png" (CC BY 4.0), rendered white.
- clio.png: derived from clio.com favicon (ring and check).
- rbc.svg: RBC Insurance site shield SVG, simplified for a 40px render: shield outline (thickened) and the RBC letters rendered white; the blue fill and the lion-and-globe interior detail were removed because they turn to noise at card size.
