# Logo sources

All marks are rendered white for use inside card art windows. Trademarks belong to their owners.

- anthropic.svg: Simple Icons (CC0) rendition of the Anthropic mark.
- qotiv.png: cropped mark from the Qotiv logo (qotiv-source.png, owned by Jaspal Singh).
- engine.png: derived from engine.com favicon (bars only).
- deel.png: Wikimedia Commons "DEEL-new-logo.png" (CC BY 4.0), rendered white.
- clio.png: derived from clio.com favicon (ring and check).
- rbc.svg: RBC Insurance site shield SVG with the blue shield fill removed; lion, letters and outline rendered white.
